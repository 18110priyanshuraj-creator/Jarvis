What are we working on?

Use the built-in offline brain by default. Optional Gemini conversation can be enabled in server settings for more open-ended replies.

Privacy: chats stay temporary in server memory for up to one hour of inactivity and disappear on sign-out or server restart. JARVIS saves only notes you explicitly ask it to remember. Relevant current questions can use live, cited web research when enabled; web results are not saved automatically.

JARVIS can make mistakes. Verify important information. External actions are not enabled.

Live research privacy and cost

Live mode is disabled by default; availability depends on server settings. When enabled, your question is sent to Google Gemini with Google Search grounding. Results show source links and are not saved automatically; use “Save research” to keep one in your private JARVIS history. Google’s Search grounding has a free monthly allowance and may incur usage charges after that. Do not enable paid billing without a parent or guardian reviewing it.
